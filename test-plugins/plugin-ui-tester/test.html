About

I am an Electrical Engineer with 4+ years of experience in machine learning and deep learning applications. My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, natural language processing, and human-computer interaction, with a focus on creating intelligent systems that serve human well-being.

I have served as a Research Assistant in the Human and Robot Interaction Laboratory, and a Teaching Assistant across Robotics, Mechatronics, and Data Analysis at the University of Tehran. My current research focus explores how adaptive rhythm-coaching systems can support post-stroke motor recovery through mobile telerehabilitation.
